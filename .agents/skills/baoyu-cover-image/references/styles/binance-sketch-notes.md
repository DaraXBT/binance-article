# binance-sketch-notes

Binance Sketch Notes — a flat black sketchbook cover drawn in gold gel-pen and off-white chalk.

**Preset mapping:** palette [`binance`](../palettes/binance.md) + rendering [`hand-drawn`](../renderings/hand-drawn.md)

## Cover Recipe

- Use a flat three-band layout: title gesture at top, 2–6 wobbly rounded cards in the middle, and takeaway gesture at bottom. Render those gestures as lettering only when text is enabled.
- Keep Canvas Black `#0C0E12` with a faint square dot grid. Gold `#F0B90B` leads; `#EAECEF` provides chalk structure.
- Give every card one topic-specific hand-drawn icon and, only when text is enabled, one short keyword. Connect cards with loose arrows and sparse stars, dots, underlines, or sparkles.
- Preserve deliberate wobble, overshoot, and imperfect rounded corners. Use at most one translucent gold-wash card.
- A single tiny hand-sketched isometric prop is optional; it must never set the perspective.

## Text and Brand

With `text: none`, preserve the three-band rhythm but replace the title and takeaway with non-lettered underline/sparkle gestures; cards contain one icon each and no keywords or numerals. When text is explicitly enabled, hand-letter all visible text in the article language. Do not draw a Binance or BNB logo.

## Avoid

Cream paper, pastel card fills, perfect geometry, sans-serif lettering, long paragraphs, center-stage isometric mechanisms, micro-people scenes, gradients, or more than one isometric accent.

## Best For

Beginner explainers, wallet and security tips, onboarding, glossary cards, quick tokenomics summaries, and friendly community education.
