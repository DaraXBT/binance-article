# binance-mondo-panoramic

Binance Mondo Panoramic — a dark screen-print cover that tells one transformation story from left to right.

**Preset mapping:** palette [`binance`](../palettes/binance.md) + rendering [`screen-print`](../renderings/screen-print.md)

## Cover Recipe

- Compose the transformation inside the centered `5:2` safe frame of the generated `2.35:1` source; use `16:9` only when explicitly requested. Divide the panorama into connected **old world → transition → new world** zones.
- Use flat symbolic objects and 2–5 grounded, zero-detail silhouettes moving through a consistent horizon.
- Keep the left zone muted in `#3D3D3D`/`#707A8A`, fracture or dissolve forms in the center, and intensify `#F0B90B`/`#FCD535` on the right.
- Apply restrained halftone dots, paper grain, and slight registration offset. Preserve dark gaps between zones as breathing room.
- Let the visual narrative work without labels. If a title is required, place it in a clean edge zone rather than over the transformation path.

## Brand

With `text: none`, omit all titles, labels, numerals, and wordmarks—the transformation must remain self-explanatory. When text is explicitly enabled, confine it to a clean edge zone. One pictorial BNB mark is optional in the new-world zone; never repeat it or turn it into a pattern.

## Avoid

Photorealism, gradients, detailed faces, disconnected zones, blue/teal/purple accents, floating silhouettes, dense text, or an old-world-only composition.

## Best For

Before/after narratives, adoption arcs, financial modernization, protocol evolution, market transformation, and cinematic article headers.
