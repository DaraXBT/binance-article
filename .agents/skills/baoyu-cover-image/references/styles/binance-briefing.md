# binance-briefing

Binance Technical Briefing — a research-grade dark cover organized around an annotated isometric figure.

**Preset mapping:** palette [`binance`](../palettes/binance.md) + rendering [`isometric`](../renderings/isometric.md)

## Cover Recipe

- Center one 30° isometric hero diagram on Canvas Black `#0C0E12`, occupying roughly 40–50% of the cover.
- Surround it with 3–6 compact callout boxes connected by dotted `#707A8A` leader lines to exact anchor points.
- Include at least one key-insight panel with a 2px `#FCD535` border and a restrained gold wash.
- When the source contains data, base bar, donut, line, or candlestick geometry on the real values; add written values only when text is enabled, and never invent decorative metrics.
- Gold leads. Teal `#2F7373`, maroon `#722F37`, and warm brown `#8B7355` may support annotations only.
- Reserve a clear top or side title zone only when text is enabled and keep the remaining dark space intentional.

## Text and Brand

With `text: none`, omit all labels and numerals; render the 3–6 callouts as iconographic micro-diagrams or chart-shape panels, and turn the key-insight panel into one gold-framed focal pictogram. Do not invent precise data that cannot be shown without text. When text is explicitly enabled, use concise labels and callouts in the article language. Do not draw a Binance or BNB logo.

## Avoid

Long paragraphs, fake data, unlabeled callout anchors, patterns above 5% opacity, teal overpowering gold, gradients, glossy effects, or a light background.

## Best For

Protocol research, tokenomics, metrics, comparisons, security analysis, whitepaper figures, and data-heavy crypto explainers.
