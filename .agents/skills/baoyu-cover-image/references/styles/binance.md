# binance

Binance Isometric Flow — a dark crypto-native cover with structured gold platforms and purposeful visual flow.

**Preset mapping:** palette [`binance`](../palettes/binance.md) + rendering [`isometric`](../renderings/isometric.md)

## Cover Recipe

- Use Canvas Black `#0C0E12` with a faint isometric dot grid and 40–60% intentional breathing room.
- Build one 30° isometric hero cluster from 1–3 floating slabs or blocks; use flat fills and ground shadows only.
- Put one topic-specific crypto icon on every major platform. Route solid `#FCD535` connectors along the isometric grid.
- Add 2–4 diverse, flat-filled micro-people only when they clarify scale or action; keep them grounded on platforms.
- Reserve a clean top or right-side title zone only when text is enabled. Gold `#F0B90B` carries the hierarchy; green/red encode data states only.

## Text and Brand

With `text: none`, omit the title zone, labels, numerals, and wordmarks; let icon placement and connectors carry the meaning. When text is explicitly enabled, use concise typeset labels in the article language. Include exactly one pictorial BNB mark, gold on dark or dark on gold; never recolor or repeat it.

## Avoid

Light backgrounds, glossy 3D, gradients, generic icons, floating people, more than three gold-topped platforms, or decoration without informational purpose.

## Best For

Crypto ecosystems, exchange mechanics, DeFi flows, blockchain architecture, trading concepts, and Web3 infrastructure.
