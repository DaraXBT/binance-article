# binance-vector-illustration

Binance Flat Vector — a front-on dark coloring-book cover with geometric toy-model props.

**Preset mapping:** palette [`binance`](../palettes/binance.md) + rendering [`flat-vector`](../renderings/flat-vector.md)

## Cover Recipe

- Compose one flat front-on hero scene on Canvas Black `#0C0E12`; create depth through overlap across no more than three layers.
- Give every illustrated shape a closed, uniform 3px rounded outline: gold `#F0B90B`/`#FCD535` for hero forms and `#EAECEF` for support forms.
- Reduce wallets, coins, charts, blocks, and interfaces to simple discs, pills, rounded rectangles, and chunky bars.
- Use outlined, diverse geometric people on a ground plane in purposeful poses. Reserve 40–60% breathing room and add a clean title zone only when text is enabled.
- At most one small isometric slab, coin stack, or block may appear as a secondary accent.

## Text and Brand

With `text: none`, omit the title zone, labels, numerals, and wordmarks; use only visual iconography. When text is explicitly enabled, use short typeset labels in the article language and never outline text glyphs. Do not draw a Binance or BNB logo.

## Avoid

Full isometric perspective, black outlines, varied stroke widths, gradients, internal shading, glossy 3D, generic icons, floating people, or office imagery without a content reason.

## Best For

Approachable DeFi education, product explainers, wallet/security how-tos, feature announcements, tokenomics walkthroughs, and beginner-friendly fintech covers.
