# isometric

Structured, clean, and playful

## Core Characteristics

Isometric viewing angle (30°), relying purely on flat color fills and ground shadows instead of actual 3D rendering. Characterized by floating platforms and structured grids.

## Lines

- Sharp straight lines aligned to an isometric grid
- No curving organic shapes where straight lines can be used
- Defined active borders (e.g. 1px line thickness) on platform surfaces
- Topic icons feature clear, outlined strokes

## Texture

- Flat filled colors over all surfaces
- No gradient or complex lighting textures
- Solely relies on ground shadows beneath the structures for separation from background

## Depth

- Created purely by orthographic projection (isometric 30° angle)
- 3 depth layers structured logically, with the foreground elements appearing larger
- Elements stack on Z-axis (platforms atop space, people atop platforms)

## Element Vocabulary

- **Platforms**: Isometric slabs, cylinders, rounded blocks.
- **Micro-people**: Flat filled, zero outlines, mixed skin tones, in purposeful poses (pointing, analyzing, building, connecting). Always standing ON platforms. No facial features required.
- **BNB mark**: 4-pointed star or 5-square diamond grid element.
- **Topic icons**: Abstract coins, charts, wallets, shields, bridges, contracts, etc., resting on platforms.
- **Data elements**: Geometric bar charts, donut charts, and connector arrows with rounded tips routing along the isometric grid.

## Typography Approach

- Clean, structured ALL-CAPS font for headings (like Bold 700)
- Metadata and subtle information in regular, highly readable typography.
- Information layout favors strict key-value pairs or concise labeling.
