# binance-briefing

Binance × Technical Briefing — dark research-grade infographic style with isometric diagrams, annotated callouts, and concise English/article-language labels

## Design Aesthetic

Research-grade technical briefing on a dark crypto-native canvas. Isometric diagrams sit at the center of information-dense but tightly organized layouts — surrounded by annotated callout boxes, concise labels in the article language, and key insight panels. Gold (#F0B90B) marks hero elements and section titles while teal (#2F7373) carries explanatory annotations and supporting diagrams. Each scene reads like a deconstructed whitepaper figure: the central isometric illustration explains the concept visually, while surrounding text boxes provide the technical context. Structured, scholarly, and unmistakably Binance.

## Background

- Color: Canvas Black (#0C0E12)
- Texture: Faint isometric dot grid in mid-tone (#2B3139); optional faded thematic background pattern at 3–5% opacity in #1E2329 — e.g. circuit traces, network nodes, or blockchain links relevant to the topic; no heavy noise

## Typography

BinanceNova (fallback: Arial). Titles in Bold 700, ALL-CAPS, gold (#F0B90B). Short labels and callout headings use the article language, Bold 700, ALL-CAPS where appropriate, in #EAECEF, #FCD535, or subordinate teal (#2F7373). Body text in Regular 400, #EAECEF, 13–14px, line-height 1.6. Key insight text in Bold 700, #FCD535, inside bordered panels. Key-value pairs: category in gold, value in white, both ALL-CAPS.

## Visual Elements

- **Isometric hero diagram**: central isometric scene at 30° angle occupying ~40–50% of canvas; flat color fills, ground shadows only — no 3D rendering; the primary visual explanation of the concept
- **Floating platforms**: slabs and blocks on iso-grid; base fill #1E2329, border 1px #2B3139, active border 1px #FCD535; hero platform top-face in gold #F0B90B; each platform carries a topic-relevant icon
- **Annotated callout boxes**: 3–6 boxes surrounding the central diagram; fill #1E2329 with 1px border in #2B3139 (inactive) or #FCD535 (key insight); each box contains a concise article-language heading and 1–3 lines of explanatory text (#EAECEF), connected to a diagram element via dotted leader line in #707A8A
- **Key insight panels**: high-emphasis bordered boxes with 2px #FCD535 border and subtle gold fill at 8% opacity; contain single-sentence takeaway in Bold #FCD535; placed at bottom of scene or sidebar
- **Topic icons**: one icon per platform matching the input theme; outlined 2px stroke; #FCD535 on dark surfaces, #0C0E12 on gold surfaces; technical-illustration style from Binance vocabulary
- **Data elements**: bar charts, donut charts, and trend lines with callout annotations explaining data points; gold bars with highlighted #FCD535 bar; connectors routing along iso-grid with solid #FCD535 rounded-tip arrows
- **Faded background pattern**: topic-relevant pattern (circuit lines, node mesh, chain links, data flow) rendered at 3–5% opacity in #1E2329 behind the main scene — adds thematic depth without visual noise
- **Micro-people**: flat filled, zero outlines, 2–4 per scene max; mixed skin tones (#5C3D2E, #C68642, #F5CBA7); scholarly poses — pointing at callouts, reading panels, presenting to each other; always standing ON platforms
- **Composition**: split or asymmetric layout — central diagram with callout boxes on 2–3 sides; triptych option for multi-concept topics (3 vertical panels); 3 depth layers with foreground larger; dark empty space is intentional; 8px base grid with all gaps ×8
- **Leader lines**: dotted 1px lines in #707A8A connecting callout boxes to their target diagram elements; lines route at 90° angles or along iso-axes; terminate with small dot at the diagram anchor point

## Style Rules

### Do

- Always use dark background (#0C0E12) — never light
- Surround central diagram with 3–6 annotated callout boxes
- Use concise labels in the article language for key terms and callouts
- Include at least one key insight panel with high-emphasis gold border
- Add faded thematic background patterns at 3–5% opacity
- Place topic-relevant icons on every platform
- Connect callout boxes to diagram elements via dotted leader lines
- Align all elements to 8px grid
- Use gold (#F0B90B) for hero elements and teal (#2F7373) for explanatory annotations
- Let dark empty space breathe — it's intentional
- Replace all nouns with input theme nouns (theme wins over style)
- Keep micro-people diverse and in scholarly/analytical poses

### Don't

- Use light gray, white, or any light background
- Leave isometric diagrams without explanatory callout boxes
- Use faded patterns above 5% opacity — they must stay subtle
- Have more than 3 gold-topped platforms in one scene
- Add decoration without informational function
- Use rounded LEGO-style fonts
- Place stamps or watermarks in corners
- Add long paragraphs or invented labels/data
- Use gradients or glossy effects on any element
- Place people floating mid-air
- Let teal overpower gold — gold leads, teal explains
- Add office/meeting imagery unless explicitly in theme

## Best For

Blockchain research articles, crypto protocol explainers, DeFi whitepapers, Web3 infrastructure breakdowns, consensus mechanism analysis, tokenomics deep-dives, cross-chain technology, Binance Research reports, crypto security analysis, and technical concept breakdowns for global audiences
