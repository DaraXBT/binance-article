# binance-mondo-panoramic

Binance × Mondo Panoramic Narrative — dark screen-print style with left-to-right evolution storytelling, silhouette figures, and structured gold accents

## Design Aesthetic

Dark-mode Mondo screen print poster meets crypto-native Binance identity. Panoramic left-to-right visual narratives on deep black canvas — each illustration tells an evolution story: old world crumbling on the left, transition zone in the center, futuristic new world glowing on the right. Silhouette figures walk through progressively changing environments. Gold (#F0B90B) is the sole hero accent, intensifying from dim on the left to bright on the right. Screen print texture with halftone dots and vintage paper grain. Bold, geometric, symbolic — never photorealistic. The result feels like a limited-edition Mondo collectible poster reimagined for Binance's crypto-native brand.

## Background

- Color: Canvas Black (#0C0E12)
- Texture: Faint halftone dot pattern using mid-tone (#2B3139), subtle vintage paper grain overlay; no gradients, no decorative noise
- Feel: Deep, cinematic, gallery-quality dark canvas

## Typography

BinanceNova (fallback: Arial). Titles in Bold 700, ALL-CAPS, gold (#F0B90B). Labels in Bold 700, ALL-CAPS, primary white (#EAECEF), +0.06em tracking, condensed sans-serif with vintage poster feel. Metadata in Regular 400, secondary gray (#707A8A), 13–14px. Titles OPTIONAL — the visual narrative should be self-explanatory without text.

## Visual Elements

- **Panoramic narrative composition** at 21:9 or 16:9 aspect ratio; story flows strictly left → right across 3 distinct zones; each zone contains its own architecture, silhouettes, and symbolic elements
- **3-zone structure**: LEFT (old world / traditional) → CENTER (transition / transformation) → RIGHT (new world / futuristic); zones separated by architectural decay, cracking walls, or dissolving elements
- **Silhouette figures**: pure black or #1E2329 filled shapes, zero internal detail, recognizable by posture and outline only; 3–5 per scene; mixed body types; walking left → right through the zones in consistent height progression; purposeful poses (carrying briefcase, mid-stride, pointing at data, building); always grounded on surfaces, never floating
- **Old-world architecture** (LEFT): ornate classical buildings, Victorian vaults, columned facades, analog clocks showing closing time, heavy vault doors, paper documents, padlocks, chains; rendered in #3D3D3D and #707A8A with minimal gold
- **Transition elements** (CENTER): cracking walls revealing circuit patterns, fragmenting architecture, broken clock pieces, paper dissolving into pixels/tokens, chains snapping, old elements morphing into geometric shapes; mix of old (#3D3D3D) and emerging new (#F0B90B)
- **Futuristic infrastructure** (RIGHT): geometric isometric platforms, holographic data screens, candlestick charts, floating geometric tokens/coins, blockchain node constellations, data streams, smart contract interfaces; bright gold (#F0B90B, #FCD535) with green (#02C076) for growth lines
- **Screen print aesthetic**: halftone dot texture overlay on all zones, slight color layer misalignment for authenticity, vintage paper grain, flat color blocks (no gradients), clean edges on silhouettes, bold contrast between elements and background
- **Symbolic objects**: each zone features 1-2 iconic objects that capture its essence (e.g., LEFT: analog clock + vault; CENTER: fragmented keys; RIGHT: glowing token + rising chart); objects tell the story without text
- **BNB mark**: optional — 4-pointed star ◆ placed once per scene on the hero platform (RIGHT zone) or as subtle element; gold on dark; never recolored
- **Composition**: strict left → right narrative flow; 3 depth layers with foreground silhouettes larger; dark empty space between zones is intentional breathing room; 8px base grid; consistent horizon line across all zones

## Illustration Zones

When used for article illustrations (non-poster), the 3-zone structure adapts to the content:

| Zone | Position | Content | Canvas % |
|------|----------|---------|----------|
| Zone L | Left ~30% | Old-world/traditional element with silhouette | ~30% |
| Zone C | Center ~20% | Transition/breaking/morphing zone | ~20% |
| Zone R | Right ~30% | Futuristic/new-world element with silhouette | ~30% |
| Breathing | Edges ~20% | Dark negative space at edges and between zones | ~20% |

**Single-zone variant:** For simpler illustrations, use only the RIGHT zone aesthetic — dark canvas with geometric isometric platforms, gold accents, and a single silhouette figure interacting with the topic's core concept.

## Style Rules

### Do

- Always use dark background (#0C0E12) — never light
- Include at least 2 silhouette figures per scene (walking through narrative)
- Create clear visual progression from old (left) to new (right)
- Use halftone dot texture and paper grain for screen print authenticity
- Let gold intensity increase from left to right
- Keep silhouettes diverse in posture and body type
- Use flat color blocks — no photorealistic rendering or gradients
- Let dark empty space breathe between zones — it's intentional
- Replace all nouns with input theme nouns (theme wins over style)
- Use symbolic objects over literal depictions

### Don't

- Use light gray, white, or any light background
- Use blue, teal, or purple as accent colors (only gold family)
- Show detailed faces on silhouette figures
- Use photorealistic rendering or complex gradients
- Overcrowd any single zone — each should have 2-3 elements max
- Add decoration without narrative function
- Use curved organic shapes — keep everything geometric and bold
- Place people floating mid-air or disconnected from surfaces
- Add text/labels to the image — let the visual narrative speak
- Make the 3 zones look disconnected — they must flow as one panoramic scene
- Use the old-world zone alone — the power is in the transformation story
