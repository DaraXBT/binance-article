# binance-sketch-notes

Binance × Sketch Notes — a dark sketchbook page hand-lettered in gold gel-pen and off-white chalk.

## Register

Keep the composition flat and front-on, never isometric-led: a title band at the top, a loose grid of 2–6 wobbly rounded cards in the middle, and one takeaway line at the bottom. At most one tiny iso accent may appear as a doodle and must never set the perspective.

## Background and palette

- Canvas Black `#0C0E12`, a subtle matte paper tooth, and a faint flat square dot grid in `#2B3139` at roughly 8px.
- Gold gel-pen `#F0B90B` and bright gold `#FCD535` lead. Off-white chalk is `#EAECEF`; surfaces are `#1E2329`; muted notes are `#707A8A`.
- One card may carry a translucent `#F0B90B` wash at 12–15%. Optional deep categorical tints are fill-only and subordinate to gold. Green/red are data states only.

## Signature rules

- All text is short, bold, mostly uppercase hand-lettering in the article language; no sans-serif or perfectly straight baselines.
- Every line, card, arrow, icon, and letter has deliberate wobble and slight painted overshoot. Use a heavier ~3px gold hero stroke and lighter ~2px chalk structure.
- Each card contains one topic-relevant Binance-vocabulary icon and one short keyword. Connect cards with loose hand-drawn arrows and sparse stars, sparkles, dots, and underlines.
- Include one short hand-lettered takeaway at the bottom. Keep the page airy and diagram-only; do not add realistic scenes or micro-people.
- Do not draw a Binance or BNB logo; the dark ground and gold/chalk hierarchy carry the brand identity.

## Do / Don't

Do keep outlines leading, use gold for hierarchy, and let dark whitespace breathe. Do not use cream paper, pastel macaron fills, perfect geometry, long paragraphs, gradients, office imagery, or more than one iso doodle.

## Best For

Beginner explainers, DeFi/Web3 onboarding, wallet and security tips, tokenomics summaries, glossary cards, and friendly campaign or feature education.
