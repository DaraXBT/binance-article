# binance-vector-illustration

Binance × Flat Vector — dark crypto-native coloring-book scenes with bold gold and light-gray outlines.

## Register

Use a single playful flat front-on hero scene on Canvas Black. Depth comes from overlap across at most three flat layers, never from isometric projection. At most one small isometric accent prop (slab, coin stack, or block) may appear, and it must remain secondary.

## Palette and shapes

- Background `#0C0E12`; flat square dot grid `#2B3139`; surfaces `#1E2329`; structure `#3D3D3D`.
- Hero/foreground outlines use `#F0B90B` or active `#FCD535`; neutral/background outlines use `#EAECEF`; recessed details may use `#707A8A`.
- Every illustrated shape has a closed, uniform 3px rounded outline. Never use black outlines, gradients, gloss, internal shading, or texture inside fills.
- Reduce objects to toy-model geometry: discs, rounded rectangles, pills, chunky bars, blocks, wallets, coins, charts, and simplified topic icons.

## People and information

People carry the same outline treatment as every other shape, use diverse flat skin tones and gold/white/gray clothing, and stand on a ground plane in purposeful poses. Use one topic-relevant Binance-vocabulary icon per major prop. Charts are chunky outlined bars, donuts, or trends; green/red are data states only. Text remains typeset, short, article-language labels without outlines on glyphs.

Do not draw a Binance or BNB logo; the dark ground, gold outlines, and crypto iconography carry the brand identity.

## Do / Don't

Do align to an 8px grid, keep gold as the only hero accent, and use playful sunbursts, clouds, stars, or dots sparingly. Do not make the scene fully isometric, use light backgrounds, vary stroke weight, float people, use generic icons, or add office/investor imagery without a theme reason.

## Best For

Approachable crypto explainers, DeFi/Web3 onboarding, tokenomics walkthroughs, wallet/security how-tos, exchange feature announcements, and community visuals.
