# binance-master

Binance × All-In-One — one gold-on-black crypto-native system with four named registers: Scene, Mechanism, Briefing, and Primer.

## Universal DNA

- Canvas Black `#0C0E12`, with a faint `#2B3139` dot grid; dark negative space is intentional.
- Flat fills only: no gradients, gloss, photorealistic rendering, or inner shading.
- Hero gold `#F0B90B`; active/CTA gold `#FCD535`; surfaces `#1E2329`; borders `#2B3139`; structure `#3D3D3D`; primary text `#EAECEF`; notes `#B7BDC6`; metadata `#707A8A`.
- Green `#02C076` and red `#F6465D` are data states only.
- Use one topic-relevant crypto icon per major element and replace generic nouns with the article's actual terms.
- Do not draw a Binance or BNB logo. Identity comes from the dark ground, gold hierarchy, and crypto iconography.
- Align gaps to an 8px grid and keep people grounded and purposeful.

## Registers

Choose exactly one register per image. Scene, Mechanism, and Briefing share a 30° isometric instrument; Primer is the only flat front-on register.

- **Scene** (default): 2–4 sparse floating platforms, topic icons, gold iso-axis connectors, and flat-filled diverse people. Use for architecture, ecosystems, frameworks, and timelines.
- **Mechanism**: one centered hero mechanism with 2–4 compact note tags, thin leader lines, and a few numbered markers. Use for a single workflow or how-it-works concept.
- **Briefing**: a dense isometric figure with 3–6 callouts, real charts, dotted leader lines, and at least one gold key-insight panel. Teal, maroon, and warm brown are subordinate annotation colors only.
- **Primer**: flat front-on toy-model composition for beginners. Every illustrated shape and person gets a uniform 3px gold or light-gray outline; depth comes from overlap, with at most one small iso accent.

## Text and data

Use typeset, concise labels in the article language. Never outline text glyphs. Briefing charts must use real article numbers and terms; never use decorative fake data. Primer may use hand-friendly geometric labels but remains typeset.

## Do / Don't

Do let the content choose the register, keep annotation proportional to the topic, use topic-specific icons, and preserve generous dark space. Do not blend registers, use a brand logo, add engineering-blue schematics, make Primer isometric, or add decoration without a function.

## Best For

Crypto/Web3 topics needing several consistent treatments: ecosystem maps (Scene), focused workflows (Mechanism), metrics and comparisons (Briefing), and warm beginner onboarding (Primer).
