# binance-fantasy-animation

Binance × Enchanted Storybook — dark crypto-native isometric style with gold-led structure, painterly warmth, and magical narrative glow

## Design Aesthetic

A distinct hybrid of Binance’s dark, disciplined isometric language and the softer emotional warmth of fantasy animation. Scenes are built on structured floating platforms over a deep black canvas, with Binance gold (#F0B90B) still leading the hierarchy. The difference is in the rendering mood: edges feel gentler, highlights glow like lantern light, surfaces carry subtle painterly texture, and characters feel more expressive and story-aware. The result should feel like a crypto-native storybook diagram — organized, premium, imaginative, and unmistakably Binance-led rather than generic fantasy.

## Background

- Color: Canvas Black (#0C0E12)
- Texture: Faint isometric dot grid in mid-tone (#2B3139) with a soft atmospheric haze or painterly wash at 3–5% opacity; no bright sky, no full fantasy landscape takeover

## Typography

BinanceNova (fallback: Arial). Titles in Bold 700, ALL-CAPS, gold (#F0B90B). Labels in Bold 700, ALL-CAPS, primary white (#EAECEF), +0.06em tracking. Metadata in Regular 400, secondary gray (#707A8A), 13–14px, line-height 1.5. Key-value pairs on same line: category in gold, value in white, both ALL-CAPS. Typography stays clean and structured, but headline areas may carry a faint warm glow or subtle brushed softness — never ornate fairy-tale lettering.

## Visual Elements

- **Isometric story scenes** at 30° angle with Binance-grade platform logic, but rendered with softer edge transitions, subtle painterly shading, and warm halo lighting; still flat-first with ground shadows only — no cinematic 3D rendering
- **Floating platforms**: slabs, cylinders, rounded blocks on iso-grid; base fill #1E2329, border 1px #2B3139, active border 1px #FCD535; hero platform top-face in gold #F0B90B; selected platforms may receive soft ember rim light or faint misty glow
- **BNB mark**: 4-pointed star ◆ or 5-square diamond grid; placed on hero platform, top-left brand bar, or as 4% opacity watermark; always one per scene; gold on dark or #0C0E12 on gold — never recolored; may carry a subtle lantern-like glow
- **Topic icons**: one icon per platform matching the input theme; outlined 2px stroke; #FCD535 on dark surfaces, #0C0E12 on gold surfaces; drawn from Binance vocabulary but with slightly softer corners and warmer highlight treatment
- **Magical atmosphere cues**: controlled glow particles, spark trails, lantern motes, or enchanted haze clustered near hero elements only; these cues support narrative warmth without breaking the composition grid
- **Characters**: flat filled, zero outlines, 2–4 per scene max; mixed skin tones (#5C3D2E, #C68642, #F5CBA7); clothing in gold, white, muted gray, and restrained ember accents; more expressive poses than base Binance (guiding, discovering, presenting, protecting), but still purposeful and always standing ON platforms
- **Companion details**: optional one small helper creature, spirit, lantern familiar, or symbolic companion when it clarifies tone or narrative; it must reinforce the concept and remain secondary to the platform scene
- **Data elements**: bar charts with gold bars and highlighted #FCD535 bar; donut charts with gold primary and structural secondary; trend lines in #FCD535 with #02C076 for rising and #F6465D for falling; connectors remain straight or iso-grid-aligned, but may have soft glow tips or painterly light bloom
- **Painterly texture accents**: subtle brushed gradients, dry-brush highlights, and warm atmospheric blending on surfaces or in the background haze; never enough to erase edges, labels, or platform readability
- **Composition**: left→right for process flow, bottom→top for hierarchy; 3 depth layers with foreground larger; max 4 platforms; dark empty space remains intentional; all spacing aligns to 8px grid; fantasy warmth lives in the rendering layer, not in loose composition

## Style Rules

### Do

- Always use dark background (#0C0E12) — never light
- Include BNB mark in every scene (one per scene)
- Place a topic-relevant icon on every platform
- Keep Binance gold (#F0B90B) as the hero accent and primary hierarchy signal
- Preserve structured isometric platform composition and 8px grid discipline
- Add softer highlights, painterly texture accents, and controlled magical glow to introduce storybook warmth
- Use expressive characters or a small companion detail only when it clarifies the concept
- Let dark empty space breathe — it is still part of the style
- Replace all nouns with input theme nouns (theme wins over style)
- Keep the final image feeling like a crypto-native narrative diagram, not generic fantasy concept art

### Don't

- Use white, cream, pastel, or any light background
- Turn the scene into generic castles, dragons, forests, or medieval fantasy unless the article theme explicitly requires them
- Let magical effects overpower data hierarchy, platform structure, or icon clarity
- Use neon rainbow accents or cool fantasy palettes as the lead color system
- Remove the topic-icon-per-platform rule
- Break grid alignment with drifting ornaments or curved decorative clutter
- Overcrowd the scene with companion creatures, props, or decorative sparkles
- Use ornate fairy-tale fonts or handwritten title lettering
- Place people floating mid-air
- Make it look like plain Binance with only a glow filter — the warmth must be visible in lighting, texture, and character charm
